# Experience artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Both images use transparent backgrounds and are illustrative artwork, not photographs from the internships. The original generated files were copied into the project without modifying their alpha channels.

- Ford: `public/experience-ford.png` (1536 × 1024)
- Skyjack: `public/experience-skyjack.png` (1536 × 1024)

## Ford prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent cutout for a dark navy engineering portfolio experience card. Subject: a modern Ford F-150 pickup truck, complete vehicle, front three-quarter view facing toward the right, recognizably Ford with a small blue oval grille badge, dark metallic silver body, dark tires. Style: premium automotive CGI editorial illustration, mostly realistic forms with slightly stylized clean sculptural surfaces, crisp detailed grille and wheels, soft cool blue rim light and warm subtle highlights. Composition: wide 3:2 canvas, complete truck centered with generous transparent margin around all edges, no cropped wheels or body, vehicle occupies 80% of canvas width, low camera angle. Background genuinely transparent. No scenery, no floor plane, no cast ground shadow, no text, no watermark, no extra vehicles. Intended to sit on a CSS blueprint grid with typography supplied by the website.

## Skyjack prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent cutout for a dark navy engineering portfolio experience card. Subject: a Skyjack red electric scissor lift machine, front three-quarter view toward the left, red base and red safety platform railings, metallic gray crossed scissor arms, four sturdy black wheels, platform modestly raised so entire machine has a compact balanced silhouette. Style: premium industrial CGI editorial illustration, mostly realistic machinery with slightly stylized clean sculptural surfaces, precise convincing mechanical details, cool blue rim light and subtle warm highlights matching an automotive render. Composition: landscape 3:2 canvas, one whole machine centered with generous transparent margin, all railings, base, and wheels visible, no cropping. Background genuinely transparent. No workers, no scenery, no floor plane, no cast ground shadow, no text, no watermark, no extra machines. Typography and decorative blueprint grid will be supplied by the website.
